# Subsystem maturity and known limitations

Assessed 2026-11-18 by reading the code and running the full test suite (Slices 1-78). Rows have been updated as features landed since then, most recently for ADR-0190 to ADR-0193 on 2026-11-22. A row is only as current as its last STATUS.md entry. The labels are deliberately conservative:

- **Production-ready**: no subsystem has this label yet. It requires a shipped or externally used game, validation on real hardware for every supported platform, and stable public APIs. None of these exist yet.
- **Usable**: implemented end to end (runtime, serialization, editor UI where relevant, scripting access), covered by automated tests, and used by a template or sample game. Expect API changes.
- **Experimental**: works and is tested, but has narrow coverage, unmeasured performance, or lacks the editor or workflow support needed for real projects.
- **Incomplete**: part of the system exists; important parts that games need are missing.
- **Missing**: not implemented.

Evidence for every item is in [STATUS.md](STATUS.md); platform coverage is in [PLATFORM_SUPPORT.md](PLATFORM_SUPPORT.md).

## Cross-cutting limitations

- **FP16 shading (ADR-0137, ADR-0153):** only the built-in PBR shader's specular BRDF terms (GGX, Smith, Schlick) have a half-precision variant (the Lambert product and the environment-BRDF fit stay FP32) (post passes and surface shaders stay FP32); it runs only on Vulkan devices with `shaderFloat16` (Metal stays FP32 until it is tested on Apple hardware); the variant clamps roughness to 0.089 instead of 0.045, so mirror highlights are broader; the speed-up has not been measured on a phone GPU.
- **Baked lighting (ADR-0122, ADR-0125):** meshes marked Static Lighting bake from `victus bake-lighting` or Studio Assets > Bake Lighting into a per-scene `.vlightmap`. The bake uses the analytic sky only (no HDRI), each material's constant base colour, multiplied for glTF model materials by the mean of the base colour texture (ADR-0145; `.vmat` textures are not sampled) and no diffuse for metals; skinned and morphing meshes are skipped, and moving a baked mesh drops it back to realtime lighting until the next bake. Bakes cover LOD0 of static meshes only, store irradiance plus one dominant direction (ADR-0130; no L1 SH) and a distance shadowmask for the sun (ADR-0131; the mask switches off when the sun moves), use RGBA16F (no RGBM/ASTC compression). Dynamic and non-lightmapped objects take their ambient from baked SH9 light probes (ADR-0133) on a regular grid of at most 32 x 16 x 32 probes; there is no adaptive density or virtual offset (probes inside geometry are dilated from their neighbours, which can leak light through walls thinner than the probe spacing), and the baked sun occlusion they give dynamic objects past the realtime shadow distance (ADR-0136) is as soft as the probe spacing; the Low tier samples them L1 only (ADR-0138) and does not draw or capture hand-placed irradiance volumes. The 3D templates bake on creation (ADR-0134), but nothing re-bakes automatically after an edit, and only congruent lightmapped copies (same mesh, same scale, any rotation) share an unwrap and instance together (ADR-0135); stretched copies keep their own. Baking is CPU-only and not yet measured on large scenes.
- **Hardware:** every rendering result was produced on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) or CI's virtualised Apple GPU. Frame-time numbers in STATUS.md are for that setup and do not predict real GPU performance.
- **Determinism:** capture/replay is verified on one compiler and machine; cross-platform floating-point determinism is not.
- **Device loss:** SDL_GPU 3.4 has no device-lost signal. VICTUS infers loss from repeated failed GPU operations. The player recreates the device and all GPU objects and keeps running (ADR-0157, up to `--device-recoveries`, default 3), then exits with code 3; the editor still autosaves and exits. Only injected failures have been tested, not a real driver reset or GPU removal. Models registered at runtime with `GpuAssetCache::add_model` are not restored, and a switch between GLES and Vulkan/Metal during recovery is refused.
- **Starter characters:** Orion and Nova (docs/CHARACTERS.md, ADR-0179) are standard metal/rough PBR surfaces: the renderer has no subsurface scattering for skin, no anisotropic hair shading and no detail-map layer, and hair is a textured scalp shell plus a bun mesh and lash cards rather than strands. There is no equipment library yet (boots, gloves, armour, backpacks; the feet wear the suit's sock), no attach API beyond the eight named socket nodes, no runtime twist or corrective-shape drivers (twist joints are skinned, clips leave them at rest), no facial layer in the shipped controller (the five face clips play only through the Animator), no authored LODs (meshoptimizer simplifications) and no Studio character workbench. Templates import their textures as BC7 and ASTC with RGBA8 kept only up to 256 px: about 130 MB of cooked data per character, stored once per machine in the shared cooked cache (ADR-0180), and about 70 MB of GPU texture memory per character on desktop; the first project on a machine spends about 25 s encoding them. Regenerating them needs Blender's `bpy` wheel (for the AO bake) and takes about six minutes per character on two cores.
- **3D template pack size:** the Orion and Nova `.glb` files (about 20 MB each) cook to about 178 MB each because the cooker keeps every texture variant. `victus export` defaults to `--target all`, so the 3D template exports a 73 MB `game.vpak` (356 MB before zstd); `--target desktop` gives 43 MB and `--target android` 23 MB (measured 2026-11-22, see PERFORMANCE_STRATEGY "Iteration times"). There is no per-project texture budget or warning yet.
- **Static analysis:** clang-tidy runs on engine, editor, tools and apps sources (see CONTRIBUTING.md), and CI fails if any tracked source of those trees is not analysed; test sources are not linted, and the job is not yet a required status check (owner-only branch-protection setting).
- **Third-party advisories:** cgltf 1.15 has four open advisories with no upstream fix. VICTUS validates accessors and unpacks sparse data itself before handing glTF data to cgltf (see THIRD_PARTY_AND_LICENSES.md); untrusted glTF files should still be treated with care.
- **Crash reports:** the handler runs inside the crashing process (ADR-0072), so heap corruption or a destroyed stack can prevent a report; there are no minidumps, no crash upload or aggregation. Linux reports are verified end to end; Windows (unhandled-exception filter) and macOS handlers are compiled in CI but not run against a real crash, Windows frames have no build-id (symbolize them with the matching .pdb), and Android reports have no stack frames. The editor does not yet start a crash session or offer "Create diagnostics bundle" (library function ready: `player::create_diagnostics_bundle`).
- **Studio samples page:** the editor finds sample projects in the source tree it was built from or in `VICTUS_SAMPLES_DIR`; installed or moved builds show an empty Samples page until that variable is set.
- **Studio guidance:** Help topics and the Getting Started checklist are built into the editor in English only; checklist progress and the interface size are per user, not per project; the size presets are fixed steps (90 % to 175 %, or any value with `--ui-scale`) and do not follow the operating system's display scaling.
- **Studio panels:** open panels, the layout preset and dock positions are remembered for the whole Studio, not per project (named layouts too).
- Acoustics (ADR-0096): occlusion models transmission through surfaces only — no diffraction around edges, no reflections, propagation or portals; each surface counts once regardless of thickness; the colour of an occluded voice is a single first-order low-pass (2.5 kHz/400 Hz tilt), so the 15 kHz band is not matched separately; reverb zones and the underwater filter act on whole buses (one listener, no per-source room); occlusion needs a bound PhysicsWorld (static/dynamic Jolt colliders), 2D physics does not occlude; no Studio preview of rays or zones yet; no HRTF.
- Low tier anti-aliasing (ADR-0188): the 0.65 render-scale threshold for switching from MSAA + FXAA to TAAU was measured on lavapipe only; no phone GPU measurements.
- Responsive blended meshes (ADR-0187): the reactive mask value is one number per material (surface shaders cannot vary it per pixel); materials with a custom vertex stage are skipped; there is no auto-generated mask, so blended meshes left at responsive 0 still smear when they move; verified on lavapipe only.
- Water (ADR-0095): volumes are axis-aligned boxes without a bottom (rotation is ignored); reflections show the environment/sky only (no planar reflections or SSR of scene objects); water receives no shadows and writes no TAA motion vectors (it writes the TAA reactive mask instead, ADR-0143, which reduces but does not remove smearing on fast waves); transparent objects under the surface draw over it; buoyancy samples one wave plane per body at its centre and uses Jolt's bounding-box volume for capsules and cylinders; 2D bodies and ragdoll bones do not float; the editor preview animates with wall-clock time; no FFT ocean spectrum yet.
- Gameplay framework (ADR-0094): runtime `Timers`, `Attributes` and `Inventory` state has no Studio inspector yet (edit `Tags`/`AttributeSet` and inspect at runtime via scripts); attributes, effects and inventories are not replicated over the network; there are no interactable/prompt components yet.
